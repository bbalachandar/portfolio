About

I'm a Full Stack Software Engineer based in Chennai, India, with a passion for building modular, scalable enterprise applications. Over the past 4 years, I've worked on everything from greenfield development to modernizing legacy platforms - always with an eye toward performance, accessibility, and code quality.

My focus is end-to-end frontend ownership: clarifying requirements with stakeholders, making sound technical decisions, leading small teams, and delivering polished solutions. I regularly contribute across the backend too - integrating REST APIs, writing microservices, and working with databases and cloud infrastructure.

I'm fascinated by modular architecture and microfrontends: the idea that large applications can be composed from independent, well-defined pieces that work seamlessly together. That thinking informs both my technical approach and my philosophy about collaboration and system design.
